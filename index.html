Why work with me

I’ve done the thing you’re trying to do.

I’m Toki — a Data Science major (Finance minor) at Tufts University, originally from Kazakhstan. I went through a U.S. high school as an international student, came in without any built-in advantage, and left with a 4.84 weighted GPA, a 1540 SAT, and enough AP credit to walk into college years ahead. I know exactly what admissions officers are looking for — because I recently convinced them to say yes.

Since then, I’ve worked in venture capital (evaluating a $300M portfolio at a top-ranked VC firm), done policy consulting in Washington, D.C., been selected for MIT’s Quantum Computing program and a NASA-funded research proposal, and founded my own national conference at Tufts. I’ve also spent college exploring — physics, economics, data science, education — because I think the best essays come from students who are genuinely curious, not ones who’ve picked a “major” at 17 and forced their story to fit it.

I’m not a generic consultant reading from a template. I’m someone who was recently exactly where you are, who speaks Kazakh and Russian natively, and who understands both the U.S. system and the specific pressure Kazakh families put on this process.
