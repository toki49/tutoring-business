Why Work With Me

I'm Togzhan — a Data Science and Economics major at Tufts University, originally from Almaty. I moved to the U.S. in elementary school and went through the college admissions process from a domestic U.S. high school, coming in with a 1540 SAT, 14 AP classes, and a 4.84 GPA. But what I'm most proud of are my essays — the stories I chose to tell, and how I told them.

At a liberal arts college, I've had the freedom to explore physics, economics, data science, and education. That range means I can speak to almost any direction you're considering, along with the kind of insider perspective on U.S. campus life that's usually only available to domestic students.

Because I went through this process myself so recently, I can meet you exactly where you are — whether that's comparing East Coast/Boston schools, narrowing down a major, understanding campus culture, or figuring out how to present your international background and activities in a way that resonates with U.S. admissions officers.
